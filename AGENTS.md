# YOYAKU automation rules

Before creating, generating or editing any `Automation` (dashboard, SQL, CSV
import, cron), read `docs/automation-playbook.md`. House style: the private DM
is sent, a public reply under the comment never is (`publicReplyEnabled=false`).
To prepare a comment-to-DM campaign from a shop product (URL or SKU), use the
campaign assistant — see `docs/campaign-assistant.md`.
